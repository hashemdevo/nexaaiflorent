# 🏛️ Component 09 — Geofencing & Logistics Transport

## 📡 Dynamic Geofence Verification
- **Haversine Math Engines**: Computes high precision distance coefficients between current employee GPS coordinates and assigned branch centers.
- **Radial Containment**: Evaluates radius thresholds configured by managers (ranging 50m to 1000m) to approve or deny biometric event compliance.

## 🚚 Transport Speed Vectors
- **Geographic Calculations**: Tracks and renders moving speeds when drivers update coordinates during transit shifts on the road.
